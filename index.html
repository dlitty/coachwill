Hi neighbors and friends! I’m William Grush, and I’m offering personalized soccer lessons in the Apex/Holly Springs area. About Coach Will: 

My first soccer experience was at age 4 in Intro to Soccer, but I’ve been playing seriously since I was 11 - playing for my Middle School team, competing with local club teams, as well as Holly Springs Parks and Rec league (twice with the All Stars).

My coaching experience began at age 16 with the Town of Holly Springs and has continued for four years now. Last year, I joined Wake Futbol Club (Wake FC) as a head coach and am heading into my second year in their U12 and younger program. Additionally, I coach the Little Kickers program for XL soccer world, a fun opportunity to share my love of the game with the youngest of players.
